In a recent December 2023 report by Comscore — the renowned platform for advertisers to gauge and analyze media engagement — Overwolf has continued to scale to the upper echelons of gaming media properties. Sitting amongst the industry giants of Twitch, Roblox, Epic Games and Activision Blizzard, Overwolf jumped 21% in unique visitors from November 2023 to December 2023, with a 19.6M reach for display/video media buying opportunities reaching gamers. The report also highlights several key facets that set Overwolf apart from its competitors.
